About Doctor AI

Project Objective

Doctor AI is a comprehensive Hospital Appointment Management System designed to bridge the gap between patients and healthcare providers. Our platform leverages modern web technologies and artificial intelligence to simplify the process of finding doctors, booking appointments, and managing healthcare needs.

Problem Statement

Traditional healthcare appointment booking systems are often inefficient, requiring patients to make phone calls, visit clinics in person, or navigate complex booking processes. Doctors struggle with managing their schedules and patient information effectively. Doctor AI addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, user-friendly platform that benefits both patients and healthcare providers.

Key Features

For Patients
  • Easy registration and profile management
  • Search doctors by specialization and location
  • View doctor profiles and availability
  • Book appointments online
  • Track appointment status and history
  • AI-powered symptom checker
  • Access medical records
For Doctors
  • Professional profile management
  • Customizable availability schedule
  • Appointment request management
  • Patient information access
  • Medical record creation
  • Appointment history tracking
  • Practice analytics

Database Design

The system uses a normalized MySQL database with the following key tables:

  • users: Authentication and user information
  • patients: Patient-specific details
  • doctors: Doctor profiles and qualifications
  • appointments: Appointment bookings and status
  • doctor_availability: Doctor schedule management
  • medical_records: Patient medical history
  • notifications: System notifications
  • ai_queries: AI assistant query history
